About

Research profile

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Padua. My current work focuses on the interaction among computation, communication, and control, especially when controllers rely on shared edge/cloud computing resources and communication networks with packet loss or delay.

Before joining the University of Padua, I was a Research Assistant at the University of Victoria. I received both my M.Eng. and B.Eng. degrees from Northwestern Polytechnical University.

Research

Research interests

Control theory

Model Predictive Control (MPC), prescribed performance control (PPC), robust control, adaptive control, optimal control, and cooperative control.

Control over wireless

Edge-based and cloud-based optimal control under packet loss and communication delay, with attention to computation-communication trade-offs.

Applications

Multi-performance flight-control requirements, cooperative control of multi-agent systems, and route planning.
