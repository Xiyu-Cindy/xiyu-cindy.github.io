Xiyu Gu

I'm currently a DPhil candidate in University of Padova, as a member of Department of Information Engineering (DEI), under the supervision of Prof. Matthias Pezzutto and Prof. Luca Schenato. My research interests include Wireless Control, Constrained Control, Prescribed Performance Control, with the applications on flight vehicles.

I welcome collaborations on related topics. If you are interested, please reach out by xiyu.gu@studenti.unipd.it.
